Method
------
Segment output ranges replicate the engine concat arithmetic exactly
(work-unit lens minus 176-sample fades), parsed from the 0.0.0.24.1 plan.txt
(work_units lens/fades); predicted totals match wav lengths EXACTLY
(9792/17446/14666), so ranges are sample-exact. Segment boundaries first read
from probe/archive/0.0.0.24/0.0.0.24.1/junctions.txt. GOAT-merged hello o+U
work unit split at first seglen (3263 samples).
Per VOICED segment interior (10 ms edges excluded, 40 ms frames / 20 ms step,
frame means - removes slow intra-segment F0-glide confound of whole-segment
autocorrelation):
 (a) voicing depth = peak normalized autocorrelation in 80-300 Hz lag band
     (73-276 samples); plus hp_depth = same metric on the 4k+ brickwall-HP
     (rfft) signal: high = HF energy is harmonic (bright formant), low = HF
     energy is inharmonic wash (noise bed).
 (b) noise-floor RMS = RMS of residual after subtracting a harmonic comb
     pitched at the lag of (a): p[i] = 0.5*(x[i-L]+x[i+L]); cross-check proxy
     hp_rms = RMS of the 4k+ brickwall-HP signal.
 (c) spectral tilt = 10*log10(E[0-1kHz]/E[4kHz+]) from rfft; HF fraction
     E[4k+]/E[total].
Across-segment variance ("different rooms" number): CV = std/mean of depth,
noise floor, hp_rms, hf_frac; std/range of tilt in dB (gain-independent:
hf_frac, tilt, hp_depth are level ratios, so scalar gain cannot explain them).
Fricatives (/h/ frication path, /s/ parallel sibilant) and /b/ burst are
REFERENCE rows, excluded from the variance verdict.

Direction for 0.0.0.31.2: UNIFORM BED (not aspiration leveling, not
per-source caps as primary). Rationale: (a) aspiration leveling is a NO-OP -
the parameter is already a constant 0.05 everywhere (citations above); there
is nothing to level. (b) Per-source caps chase 5+ branches while the voiced
set shares one constant source - the variance is emergent downstream, so cap
the EMERGENT quantity, not the branches. (c) Tilt matching by EQ risks
dulling phonetic brightness (formants ARE the vowel); a common low-level
shaped bed UNDERNEATH preserves phonetics while compressing the max/min bed
ratio from below (e.g. bed at hf~0.0003 turns hello's 6x into ~2.3x). Gate it
with the per-segment HF-fraction variance gate already foreseen in
0.0.0.31.2, computed on long stable segments / frame-medians per the
halves-stability warning. Prime suspect for the injector (to confirm in
0.0.0.31.2): the peak tamer (synth.rs:1053-1068), the only causal stage that
adds HF conditionally per segment by construction (crest-factor-dependent
clipping); test by toggling tamer vs bed and watching hf_frac CV.
