Why the fix cannot work (two independent blockers)
--------------------------------------------------
1. Blend-weight proof. The short-fade crossfade (synth.rs:740-744) is
out[at] = out[at]*(1-t) + s*t with t = k/n. At k = 0 (the window start)
the new segment has weight exactly 0: the output sample is 100% previous
segment content, mathematically invariant under any new-filter state.
Measured argmax sits at exactly d = 0 for hello b2/b3, yeobo b5 and most
Korean boundaries - those peaks are previous-segment ends (loud vowel
offsets, /s/ bodies), not fresh-filter onsets. Small-d>0 peaks (hello b1
d=1.8 ms, annyeong b9 d=1.3 ms) sit 3-16% into the blend and are likewise
previous-content dominated; warming the new filter moves <=22% of the mix
and measured zero. Resonator memory (~3-4 ms at 80-120 Hz bandwidths) is
also shorter than the 8 ms crossfade, so by the post-blend region the
filter has converged regardless of priming.
2. Out-of-scope mechanisms own the residue. The remaining overshoot is the
phase-0 excitation restart hitting leveler onset makeup (up to +16 dB on
quiet onsets), plus phonetic /s/ level at s->e boundaries (annyeong b9,
yeobo b5: full-scale /s/ bodies straddling the boundary inside the window
by design, smoothing contraindicated per 0.0.0.24.1). Excitation, leveler,
gain, and spectral shaping are all explicitly frozen by this task.
A weak-tail prime (h->E: -10 dB noise tail) is additionally near-identical
to zero state, so even the first 2 ms trajectory barely changes.
