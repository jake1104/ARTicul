Normalization design (crates/articul-cli/src/synth.rs, no source comments)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
One new function, applied at both blend assembly sites (one mechanism):
- normalize_blend_energy(side_a, side_b, mixed) (synth.rs:717): for each
  mixed sample out = (1-t)*a + t*b with t = k/n, compute a cap-only slow
  gain g[k] = min(1, target[k]/local[k]). target[k] = ra^(1-t)*rb^t, the
  geometric interpolation of the two sides' full-blend-window RMS
  (ra, rb; 1e-12 floor inside sqrt). local[k] = RMS of mixed over a
  centered boxcar of width w = clamp(n/4, 32, 220) (44 samples / 2ms for
  the n=176 short blends; 220 for n=1102 VV glides). If either side RMS
  is below 1e-6 (pause/silence side), the blend is returned untouched.
  Pure attenuation, deterministic, no state.
- Glide path (filter_glide re-filter output, synth.rs:772): side A is the
  truncated-away out tail captured BEFORE truncate (side_a to_vec,
  synth.rs:769-771), side B is seg head; the glide output vector is
  normalized, then extended. Bug found during implementation: indexing
  side A after truncate reads the wrong (pre-blend) content and panics
  when the truncated tail is shorter than n (determinism
  glide_regions_stay_finite caught it on "oU asa"); fixed by capturing
  side A before truncate; determinism re-passes.
- Linear path (in-place crossfade of shaped outputs, synth.rs:786): sides
  via split_at_mut around the blend region, normalized in place.
- Untouched: gains/gain track, filter tables, F0, excitation, leveler,
  tamer, ceiling/final 1.25x/clamp, fades, pause logic, envelopes.
  GOAT merge internal interpolation (render_diphthong per-sample retune)
  is NOT served by the blend function and is untouched. The VV glide path
  (filter_glide, n=1102) IS served by concat_formant_glide and is covered
  by the same mechanism. None of the three gate utterances contain a VV
  glide (all blends n=176; GOAT pairs are pre-merged).

What the residue looks like (why the gate cannot pass under frozen mechanisms)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stage analysis pre-fix (stage-compare.log, gain-probe.log): the failing
final peaks sit on 8-10x leveler makeup (hello s1 radiated 0.1159 ->
final 0.9480, gain 8.18x; s2 radiated 0.0949 -> 0.9360, gain 9.87x;
annyeong s9 radiated 0.3306 -> 0.9806, gain 2.97x). Two structural facts
make slow RMS normalization inert against this, both confirmed by
measurement rather than theory alone:
(a) At the failing peak positions (d = 0.3/1.7/2.4 ms, i.e. t = 0.04-0.3
into the 8 ms blend) the mixed content is side-dominated, so local mixed
RMS approximately equals the geometric target there and g ~= 1 BY
CONSTRUCTION. The peaks are crest (peak/RMS), not energy: slow gain
scaling preserves crest, and the frozen leveler converts crest back into
absolute peaks.
(b) The frozen leveler owns slow energy end to end: quiet-window blends
sit in its upward-expansion region (0.85 slope below the -12 dB knee),
which recompense attenuation, and the final 1.25*0.989/maxv peak pinning
globally rescales everything whenever a clamped maximum is removed
(whack-a-mole: post-fix s1 clamped 4->3 and s2 unclamped ROSE
0.9360->0.9749; annyeong s4 rose 0.2083->0.3070 purely through leveler
recompute, its own blend gain being 1). Any slow-gain blend fix is fought
by these two frozen stages; fixing the metric needs leveler-scope
authority, which this task explicitly freezes.
Phonetic /s/-body edges (annyeong s9, yeobo s5) remain hot by design and
are untouched in crest terms by this mechanism.

Revert section (recovery round, coordinator decision recorded)

lives in leveler scope, not in blend arithmetic: the failing peaks ride
8-10x frozen leveler makeup on side-dominated content (gain-probe.log:
hello s1 8.18x, s2 9.87x), the cap-only slow gain is ~=1 at every failing
