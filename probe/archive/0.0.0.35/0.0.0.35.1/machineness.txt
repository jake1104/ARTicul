(a) F0 jitter, period-to-period (sustained `a`, seg1 interior, F0 ~149Hz)
- Construction-exact (numpy replication of source.rs phase accumulation incl. lcg11 seed):
  local jitter = 0.042% (F0=120) / 0.044% (F0=149), lag-1 of period series = -0.08 / -0.01
  (white, no temporal structure). Filter is LTI so waveform timing inherits this exactly.
- Natural: male /a/ mean 0.49-0.50% (Felippe et al. 2006, CSL, n=40); healthy Praat ~0.3%;
  MDVP pathology threshold 1.04% (Praat manual notes true threshold lower).
  Task band 0.5-1.0%.
- Deficit: engine ~12-24x BELOW natural; spectrum white (lag1 ~ 0) vs natural 1/f plus
  2-5 Hz microtremor peak, and tremor perturbations LARGER than jitter (Schoentgen 2003).
- Rejected artifacts: naive peak-picking gave 2.5% (formant-ripple misassignment + glide);
  brickwall zero-crossing gave 1.2% even on a pure tone (short-window edge ringing).

(b) Shimmer, per-period amplitude (same region)
- Construction-exact: shim redrawn once per cycle as 1+0.03*U(-1,1) -> local shimmer
  = 0.03*E|U1-U2| = 0.03*2/3 = 2.0% (~0.17 dB mean-abs).
- Natural: MDVP local < 3.81%; Fernandez Liesa et al. 1999: 3% excludes 13% of healthy;
  Felippe male 0.22-0.23 dB; task band ~3%.
- Verdict: low-normal, IN range. Not a driver. (Waveform-measured 8-18% values were
  boundary/silence contamination, rejected.)

(c) Harmonic phase coherence (vowel center, 50ms frames / 5ms hop, k=1..8)
- Metric: frame-to-frame residual of harmonic phase after removing predicted
  evolution 2*pi*k*F0*hop/sr (lag-1 circular std; pure cycle shifts cancel, so this
  measures pulse-SHAPE change + noise only).
- Engine: mean 0.33 rad; k1 0.12, k2 0.27, k3 0.19, k4 0.27, k5 0.37, k6 0.42, k7 0.48,
  k8 0.57 rad - rising with k = breathiness-noise floor (cycle-gated aspiration noise
  randomizes high harmonics more). k1 residual 0.12 rad = relative phases near-locked.
- Construction: lf_pulse() shape fixed per segment; the SAME pulse replayed every cycle,
  so relative phases are locked by design; the 0.33 rad is the gated-noise floor only.
- Natural per literature: no cycle repeats - cycle-to-cycle dispersion is exactly what
  jitter (0.5-1.0%), shimmer (~3%) and microtremor (0.4-1.3%) quantify; pulse shape
  (OQ/asymmetry/speed quotient) wanders every cycle.
- Buzziness: Klatt 1980 - impulse-train source sounds "unpleasantly mechanical" as F0
  rises; Childers & Lee 1991 ("voice sounds buzzy (Sambur et al. 1978)" when natural
  excitation absent); Cabral et al. 2007 - pulse train gives buzzy HMM quality, mixed-band
  excitation + phase manipulation reduces it; Klatt 1987 - fixed source over a sentence
  gives "stereotyped voice quality".

(d) Within-segment formant drift (F1/F2 per 30ms, peak-pick + band centroid)
- Demo final-/o/ interiors with replicated Korean plan bounds (drift 0/-1 samples vs
  render): annyeong [24216,28693) + yeobo [14312,18877): F1-centroid range 3.2 Hz
  (std 1.16), F2-centroid range 1.5 Hz (std 0.64), 4 frames each. Bit-static.
  (Both demos agree to 0.01 Hz - expected: deterministic engine, same vowel/voice/context.)
- Construction: filter coefficients retuned only at boundaries (per-sample lerp confined
  to blend/overlap zones); OQ/asymmetry/aspiration fixed per segment.
- Contrast: cross-boundary F2 jumps up to ~1450 Hz (0.0.0.29.1) -> stepwise-hold
  ("piano-key") articulation: dead still within segments, leaps between them.
- Natural: articulators never hold still; sustained phonation carries 4-8 Hz tremor-rate
  modulation (Shipp & Izdebski 1981: 4-8 Hz acoustic oscillation in vibrato/tremor).

(e) F0 microprosody below phrase/accent commands
- Solo seg1, linear glide removed (slope 4.06 st/s): residual std = 0.067 st (n=11) =
  estimator floor. Engine has NO sub-syllabic F0 layer: within-segment F0 is analytic
  raised-cosine/exp glide only; per-sample white FM averages to ~0.04%.
- Natural: vocal microtremor IQR 0.4-1.3% depth at 2.0-4.7 Hz (Schoentgen 2002, JASA;
  0.4-1.3% ~= 0.07-0.22 st peaks) plus drift; tremor perturbations exceed jitter
  (Schoentgen 2003, J Voice).
- Demos after 200ms-median detrend: hello 1.91 st (nuclear accent curvature), Korean
  0.33-0.36 st = AP-command curvature leaking through the median window, not microprosody.

Ranking (by measured gap x perceptual weight; Kreiman & Gerratt 2003: listeners are
near-insensitive to jitter/shimmer amounts but hear noise/buzz - weight accordingly)
1. (c) Phase-locked identical pulse - residual 0.33 rad noise floor; classic Klatt buzz.
   Biggest TIMBRE contributor. Gap: shape variation exactly zero by construction.
2. (a) Jitter 0.042% vs 0.5-1.0% (12-24x low) + white (lag1~0) vs 1/f + tremor peak.
   Biggest NUMERIC deficit; couples into the same fix.
3. (e) Microprosody ~0.07 st floor vs tremor 0.07-0.22 st + drift; no sub-syllabic layer.
   Couples with the tremor half of the fix.
4. (d) Static within-segment filters/OQ (3 Hz drift / 200 ms) vs continuous movement.
   Separate (filter-side) mechanism - next cascade, not this fix.
5. (b) Shimmer 2.0% vs ~3% - low-normal; not a driver.

Verdict: SINGLE biggest contributor = (c) the bit-identical replayed glottal pulse
(harmonic phase coherence). 0.0.0.35.2 direction: per-cycle source variation - perturb
pulse SHAPE per cycle (OQ/asymmetry wander) together with natural-depth cycle jitter
(~0.7%) and a 2-5 Hz microtremor component (~0.8%), single source-side mechanism that
also closes (a) and (e) as coupled symptoms. TDD gates: jitter local 0.5-1.0% on the
`solo_a_nospace` interior, k1 phase residual clearly above the 0.12 rad floor,
linear-detrended microprosody std >= tremor floor, shimmer ~3%, TIs TIN hash re-measured.
