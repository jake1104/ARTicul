Method (probe/0.0.0.25.1/sparks.py, python3 + numpy, stdlib wav I/O)
------------------------------------------------------------------
crest[i] = peak(|x|, centered 2 ms window) / rms(x, centered 20 ms window),
1 ms hop. flag[i] = crest[i] > 4.0 * median(crest[i-100 .. i-1]) AND peak >= 0.01
(-40 dBFS floor keeps flat-digital-silence math out; no other gating).
Consecutive flagged frames grouped into sparks. Dominant band per spark from
1024-pt FFT of the centered 20 ms window vs the 100 ms background before it:
reported both as max energy-RATIO band (relative HF lift) and absolute excess
band (excess_b = spark_b - bg_b * total_scale, i.e. where the extra joules sit).

Verdict
-------
Sparks cluster at segment starts: 8/10 within +-10 ms of tabled boundaries at
the 2.5x operating point (10/10 including the seg-0 onset pair), all 3 hello
boundaries carry a full-scale 0.989 peak at d <= 0.9 ms, worst sharp spark
annyeong b6 N->h ratio 3.08 amp 0.4335 dur 2 ms band 8k+(x11.9), longest
annyeong b11 j->o dur 4 ms amp 0.7608, loudest hello b2/b3 amp 0.9890 dur 1-2 ms.
Positional evidence implicates suspect (a): fresh filter per segment +
phase-0 excitation restart -> transient overshoot at segment starts, capped
(not caused) by the tamer at ~2.2x - which is why the prescribed 4x bar sees
only the onset event. MIXED-LINEAR crossfade (c) and creak (d) are exonerated
with numbers; the tamer (b) is a mitigator.
Single fix for 0.0.0.25.2: filter-state carry across same-filter boundaries
(or segment-start fade-in). Gate on the 2.5x family: spark count/peak must drop
while `TIs TIN` hash is re-measured in-task.
