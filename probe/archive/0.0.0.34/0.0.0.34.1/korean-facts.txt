(b) Seoul AP intonation (Jun). K-ToBI conventions (Jun, UCLA K-ToBI site; Jun 1993 diss; Jun 1998
Phonology 15.2; Jun 2005.ch): prosodic units IP > AP; AP tone THLH (T=H iff AP-initial segment
aspirated/tense, else L), most common surface LHLH/HHLH (4+ syllables); AP-final boundary Ha =
RISE (LHa label); IP-final boundary takes 1 of 9 tones (L%, H%, LH%, HL%, ...), AP-final H
overridden IP-finally. IP-final syllable lengthened ~1.6-2x (Jeon 2014 citing review: 1.6-1.8;
Jun 2005: "about twice"). NO lexical stress or pitch accent in Korean; prominence is phrasal
(Jun & Lee 1998: focus initiates a new AP, not a +5st accent). Greeting contour: polite
greetings/questions take rising boundaries — H% (yes/no-question rise), LH% (question/
continuation/explanatory rise in declaratives), HL% (polite declarative) per Jeon, H.-S. (2014),
Speech Prosody boundary-tone perception review of Jun/Lee; pedagogical sources concur:
여보세요 opener "on a rising tone" (elon.io phone-call grammar), 안녕하세요 "calm and neutral",
English-style emphatic intonation "sounds unnatural" (90daykorean). I.e. canonical
안녕하세요/여보세요 = gentle AP rise-fall + HIGH or politely-falling (HL%) ending, NEVER a
+5st nuclear spike followed by an 80 Hz creaky -7st drop (that maps to L% as-a-matter-of-fact
declarative, Jeon 2014).
(c) Consonants. Lenis voicing: lenis stops voice intervocalically WITHIN an AP (Jun 1993, 1995,
1996; blocked across AP boundary; gradient across/inside speakers, males voice more: 2021
focus study, koreascience JAKO202009135472559). Demos: ㅂ in 여보세요 is intervocalic onset ->
[b] CORRECT as rendered (input b). ㅎ in 하세요 is intervocalic -> [ɦ] CORRECT as rendered
(input h\, M2). /s/ stays canonically voiceless intervocalically (standard lenis-voicing rule
covers stops/affricates; youngkorean KAEPS summary; Kim diss. notes gradient partial voicing
in some tokens — no action). Liquid /l/: single phoneme; onset = flap [ɾ], coda/geminate =
lateral [l] (Crosby & Dalola 2021 PLSA; Heo & Lee 2004 WPL; Lee 1999). NO /l/ in these demos
-> no action (flag for future Korean inputs containing ㄹ).

P1. AP prosody rule (Korean path only; all values in semitones relative to voice base f0 = 120 Hz; copy verbatim):
P1 Stress-mark handling: '"' and '%' are parsed normally but IGNORED in the Korean path. Stress multiplier forced to 1.0 for every unit. '"'/'%' create NO Primary/Secondary accent and have NO duration effect.
P2 Nucleus/downstep replacement: NONE. No nucleus search, no +5.0st accent, no -1.5st downstep, no post-nuclear 0.5^d tail. All vowel F0 comes from the AP contour (P3-P5) plus CF0 (P6) only.
P3 Phrase accent shape (THLH; linear interpolation between syllable anchors):
  T (syllable 1): +1.0st if AP-initial segment is aspirated/tense (s, h, h\) [H variant], else -1.0st [L variant].
  H (syllable 2; APs with >=3 syllables, else merged): +1.5st.
  L (penult; APs with >=4 syllables, else the pre-final available syllable): -0.5st.
  Short-AP mapping: 1-syllable AP = single anchor 0.0st (boundary tone only); 2-syllable AP = T then Ha; 3-syllable AP = T, H, Ha (L skipped).
P4 AP-final Ha rise: +2.0st above the L anchor on the AP-final syllable (Ha = -0.5 + 2.0 = +1.5st when L is present; +1.5st absolute for short APs), applied if and only if the AP has >=3 syllables. APs with <3 syllables carry the boundary tone only (no independent Ha step).
P5 IP-final boundary tone (utterance-final syllable; replaces the Ha target):
  L% (declarative default): -3.5st relative to base, modal voicing REQUIRED (creak path disabled on the Korean voice), hard floor: final F0 clamped to >=100 Hz.
  H% (greeting/question default; BOTH shipped demos use H%): +3.0st relative to base, modal voicing, no creak.
P6 CF0: UNCHANGED (+10% onset decay for dur>=50ms, else flat +5%; universal phonetic effect, not a foreignness cue).
P7 Durations: stress factor forced 1.0 for all units; initial 1.1 KEPT (AP-initial strengthening, Cho & Keating); final factor 1.3 REPLACED by 1.6 on the IP-final syllable only (Jeon/Jun 1.6-1.8 range, lower bound); ctx/poly/cluster/variance factors unchanged.
